# Nexli Funding Sales Scripts & Conversation Frameworks

## Overview

This directory contains comprehensive sales scripts and conversation frameworks for Nexli Funding's 8-workflow sales process. These scripts are designed to help sales teams maximize conversions at every stage of the customer journey.

## Core Philosophy

**The Nexli Approach: Speed, Trust, Results**
- Strike while interest is hot (call within 4-7 minutes)
- Build trust through transparency and expertise
- Focus on helping businesses succeed, not just closing deals
- Use proven psychological triggers while maintaining ethical standards

## How To Use These Scripts

### 1. **Phone Scripts** - Your Primary Weapon
- Memorize the opening and closing for each workflow stage
- Use the conversation flow as a guide, not a rigid script
- Adapt language to match the prospect's communication style
- Always sound conversational, never robotic

### 2. **Objection Handling** - Your Shield
- Study the common objections for each workflow stage
- Practice the responses until they become natural
- Use the "Feel, Felt, Found" technique when appropriate
- Always acknowledge the prospect's concern before responding

### 3. **Email Sequences** - Your Follow-Up Artillery
- Use these for prospects who don't answer phone calls
- Customize the templates with specific prospect details
- Send at optimal times (Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM - 2 PM)
- Always include a clear call-to-action

### 4. **SMS Sequences** - Your Quick Strike Force
- Perfect for busy prospects who prefer text
- Keep messages under 160 characters when possible
- Use immediately after missed calls
- Include your direct phone number for callbacks

## Workflow-Specific Guidelines

### WF-1: Initial Contact (NEW → WORKING)
**Primary Goal**: Get document upload commitment within first call
**Key Metric**: 60%+ should move to WORKING stage
**Best Practice**: Stay on phone until docs are uploaded

### WF-2: Document Chase (WORKING → DECISION)
**Primary Goal**: Complete document collection within 72 hours
**Key Metric**: 70%+ should provide all required documents
**Best Practice**: Use urgency without pressure tactics

### WF-3: Lender Submission (Internal Process)
**Primary Goal**: Package and submit within 8 hours
**Key Metric**: Average processing time under 8 hours
**Best Practice**: Communicate proactively with prospects during process

### WF-4: Offer Review (DECISION → FUNDED)
**Primary Goal**: Close within 2 hours of offer presentation
**Key Metric**: 45%+ acceptance rate on qualified offers
**Best Practice**: Create urgency while being transparent about terms

### WF-5: Post-Funding (FUNDED → Reviews/Referrals)
**Primary Goal**: Secure reviews and referrals within 7 days
**Key Metric**: 30%+ review rate, 20%+ referral rate
**Best Practice**: Strike while satisfaction is highest

### WF-6: Long-Term Nurture (Re-engagement)
**Primary Goal**: Reactivate cold leads with new interest
**Key Metric**: 15%+ re-engagement rate per quarter
**Best Practice**: Provide value before asking for anything

### WF-7: Credit Repair/Disqualification
**Primary Goal**: Maintain relationship for future opportunities
**Key Metric**: 25%+ eventually qualify for products
**Best Practice**: Position as helpful advisor, not just salesperson

### WF-8: Calendar Management
**Primary Goal**: Maximize show-up rate for scheduled appointments
**Key Metric**: 80%+ show rate for confirmed appointments
**Best Practice**: Multiple touchpoints without being annoying

## Sales Psychology Principles

### The NEXLI Framework
- **N**urture relationships first, sales second
- **E**mpathize with business owner challenges
- **X**ecute with urgency and professionalism
- **L**isten more than you talk
- **I**nvest in long-term customer success

### Key Psychological Triggers
1. **Urgency**: Limited time offers, same-day processing
2. **Social Proof**: Success stories, testimonials, volume stats
3. **Authority**: Industry expertise, lender relationships
4. **Reciprocity**: Free credit reports, business advice
5. **Scarcity**: Limited funding available, exclusive rates

## Compliance Guidelines

### Financial Services Regulations
- Never guarantee approval or specific rates
- Always disclose that rates and terms may vary
- Include required disclaimers in written communications
- Document all interactions for regulatory compliance

### Communication Best Practices
- Identify yourself and company at start of every call
- Respect Do Not Call lists and opt-out requests
- Use clear, truthful language about products and services
- Never make false or misleading statements

## Performance Tracking

### Key Metrics to Monitor
- **Conversion Rate**: % moving from each stage to next
- **Call-to-Close Time**: Average time from first call to funding
- **Objection Resolution**: % of objections successfully handled
- **Customer Satisfaction**: Post-funding satisfaction scores

### Daily Activities Checklist
- [ ] Review pipeline priorities (DECISION → NEW → WORKING)
- [ ] Complete all DECISION stage calls first
- [ ] Follow up on all NEW leads within 1 hour
- [ ] Address all WORKING stage leads over 48 hours old
- [ ] Send end-of-day SMS to unreachable prospects
- [ ] Update CRM with all call outcomes and next steps

## Training Recommendations

### Week 1: Foundation
- Memorize phone scripts for each workflow
- Practice objection handling responses
- Shadow experienced reps on live calls

### Week 2: Application
- Make 50+ calls using the scripts
- Record yourself and review for improvement
- Focus on natural delivery and conversation flow

### Week 3: Mastery
- Handle objections without referring to scripts
- Customize approaches based on prospect personality
- Achieve target conversion rates for each workflow

### Week 4: Optimization
- Develop your own variations that maintain effectiveness
- Mentor newer team members
- Contribute feedback for script improvements
