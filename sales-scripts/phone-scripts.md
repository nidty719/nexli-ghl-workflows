# Nexli Funding Phone Scripts - Complete Conversation Frameworks

## Introduction

These scripts provide conversation frameworks for each stage of the Nexli Funding sales process. They are designed to be natural, conversational, and effective while maintaining compliance with financial services regulations.

**Remember**: These are frameworks, not rigid scripts. Adapt your language and approach based on the prospect's communication style and personality.

---

## WF-1: Initial Contact Script (NEW → WORKING)

### Purpose: Convert fresh applications to document submission

**Timing**: Call within 4-7 minutes of application submission  
**Goal**: Get prospect to upload documents during the call  
**Target Conversion**: 60%+ to WORKING stage

### Opening (First 30 seconds)
```
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from Nexli Funding. I'm calling because I just saw your application for business funding come through our system. 

[Pause for acknowledgment]

First, congratulations - our AI system pre-qualified your application, which means you're approved to move forward with funding options. 

Do you have just a couple minutes to discuss how we can get you funded this week?"
```

### If they say YES - Continue to Qualification
```
"Perfect. Just to confirm, you applied for [funding amount] for [business purpose], is that correct?

[Wait for confirmation]

Great! Based on your application, you've been pre-qualified for [specific funding options]. The good news is we can potentially have funds in your account within [timeframe based on product].

To move forward, I need to get some documents from you - and the best part is, I can help you upload them right now while we're on the phone. It takes about 5 minutes.

Are you near a computer or smartphone where you can access your documents?"
```

### If they say NO - Schedule Callback
```
"I completely understand. When would be a better time for me to call back? I want to make sure we don't lose your pre-qualification status.

[Get specific time]

Perfect, I'll call you at [time] on [date]. In the meantime, I'm going to send you a text with some information about your pre-qualification. 

Just so you know, these pre-qualifications are time-sensitive, so the sooner we can connect, the better. Is this the best number to reach you at?"
```

### Document Collection Flow
```
"Excellent. I'm going to send you a secure link via text message right now. 

[Send the link]

Did you receive that text? Great. Click on the link and it will take you to our secure portal.

Based on your funding option, here's what we need:

[For Revenue Loan/MCA]:
- Last 4 months of business bank statements
- Copy of your government-issued ID
- A voided business check

[For Credit Stacking]:
- Government-issued ID
- I'll help you get your credit report through our partner

The upload process is really simple - just take photos with your phone camera. I'll stay on the line while you do this to answer any questions.

Can you start with your ID? Just take a clear photo of the front..."
```

### Staying on Phone During Upload
```
"Great, I can see that coming through on my end. Now let's get those bank statements. You'll want the last 4 complete months...

[Guide them through each document]

Perfect! I can see all your documents coming through. Our underwriting team will review these within the next few hours, and I'll personally call you as soon as we have an update.

Based on what I'm seeing, you should hear back from us by [specific time]. If anything additional is needed, I'll call you directly."
```

### Closing
```
"[Name], you've been fantastic to work with. I've moved your application to our priority review queue. 

You should expect a call from me or one of my colleagues within [timeframe] with your funding decision.

Is this the best number to reach you at? Great.

One last thing - do you have any questions about the process or what happens next?

[Answer questions]

Perfect. Thank you for choosing Nexli Funding, and I'll be in touch very soon with your decision."
```

## WF-2: Document Chase Script (WORKING → DECISION)

### Purpose: Complete document collection within 72 hours

### Day 1 Follow-Up (If documents incomplete)
```
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from Nexli Funding. I wanted to follow up on your application from yesterday.

I can see that we received [list documents received], but we're still missing [list missing documents] to complete your file.

The good news is our underwriters are standing by to review your complete application as soon as we get these last pieces. 

Are you able to upload those remaining documents now? I can stay on the phone to help if needed."
```

### Day 2 Follow-Up (Urgency building)
```
"Hi [Name], [Your Name] from Nexli Funding again. 

I want to make sure we don't lose your pre-qualification. We're still missing [documents] from your application, and our underwriting window closes at [specific time] today.

I have your file flagged as priority, but I need those documents to get you in front of the lenders today.

What's preventing you from uploading those documents right now? Let me see if I can help solve that."
```

### Day 3 Final Attempt
```
"[Name], this is [Your Name] from Nexli Funding. This is my final attempt to reach you about your funding application.

Your pre-qualification expires at midnight tonight, and I'd hate to see you lose this opportunity.

If you're no longer interested, I understand - just let me know and I'll close your file.

But if you still need funding, I need those documents within the next few hours. 

Can you help me understand what's going on? Is there something I can do to make this easier for you?"
```

## WF-4: Offer Review Script (DECISION → FUNDED)

### Purpose: Close the deal within 2 hours of offer presentation

**This is your money call - prepare mentally before dialing**

### Opening (High energy, excited)
```
"[Name]! This is [Your Name] from Nexli Funding, and I have fantastic news for you.

[Pause for suspense]

You've been approved! 

[Wait for their reaction]

The lender has approved you for [amount] with [brief terms]. I have the offer details right here, and I need to walk you through them right now because this offer expires at [specific time] today.

Do you have a few minutes to go through the details?"
```

### Presenting the Offer
```
"Here are your approved terms:

- Funding Amount: $[amount]
- [For Term Loan]: Fixed monthly payment of $[amount] for [term]
- [For MCA]: Daily payment of $[amount] based on [percentage]% of daily sales
- Funding Timeline: Money in your account within [timeframe]

The rate works out to [explain rate simply] which is competitive for this type of funding.

What questions do you have about these terms?"
```

### Urgency Creation (Ethical)
```
"I need to be transparent with you - this offer is only good until [time] today. The lender has limited funding available at these rates, and they need a decision quickly.

If you're ready to move forward, I can get the contracts sent to you right now, and we can have your funding as early as tomorrow.

What do you think? Are you ready to accept this offer?"
```

### If They Say YES
```
"Excellent decision! I'm sending the contracts to your email right now. 

[Send contracts]

The process is simple - you'll receive an email with an electronic signature link. Review the terms, and if everything looks correct, sign and return.

Once I receive your signed contracts, I'll immediately notify the lender, and your funding will be processed.

Can you check your email now and confirm you received the contracts?"
```

### If They Say MAYBE
```
"I understand this is a big decision. What specific concerns do you have? Let me see if I can address them.

[Listen to concerns, address each one]

I want to make sure you're completely comfortable with this decision. These terms are really competitive for your situation, and the funding will help your business [relate to their stated goals].

What would it take for you to feel confident moving forward today?"
```

### If They Say NO
```
"I respect that. Can you help me understand what's not working about this offer?

[Listen carefully]

I see. Let me ask you this - what would the ideal terms look like for your situation?

[Listen to their response]

Let me see what I can do. I can't make any promises, but I might be able to go back to the lender with some modifications. 

Would you be interested if I could [specific improvement they mentioned]?"
```

### Closing After Acceptance
```
"[Name], congratulations on your funding approval! You made a smart decision for your business.

Here's what happens next:
1. You'll receive a funding confirmation email within [timeframe]
2. Your funds will be in your account by [specific date/time]
3. I'll personally follow up to make sure everything goes smoothly

You also have my direct number - [phone] - if any questions come up.

It's been a pleasure working with you, and I know this funding is going to help take your business to the next level."
```

## General Call Management Tips

### Handling Voicemail
```
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from Nexli Funding calling about your business funding application. 

I have some important information about your pre-qualification that I need to share with you today.

Please call me back at [phone number]. Again, that's [repeat number slowly].

I'll also follow up with a text message so you have my information.

Looking forward to hearing from you."
```

### Handling Gatekeepers
```
"Hi, this is [Your Name] from Nexli Funding. [Business Owner Name] is expecting my call regarding their approved funding application. Is he/she available?"

[If they ask for details]

"It's regarding the business funding application they submitted. This is time-sensitive information about their approval status."
```

### Managing Call Time
- Keep NEW calls under 15 minutes
- WORKING calls should be 5-7 minutes
- DECISION calls can take 20-30 minutes
- Always respect their time but don't rush the close

### Voice Tone Guidelines
- **NEW calls**: Enthusiastic, congratulatory
- **WORKING calls**: Helpful, slightly urgent
- **DECISION calls**: Confident, excited
- **Nurture calls**: Friendly, no-pressure

---

**Remember**: Practice these scripts until they feel natural. The goal is confident, conversational delivery that builds trust and moves prospects forward in the process.
