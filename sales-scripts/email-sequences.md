# Nexli Funding Email Sequences - Advanced Follow-Up Scripts

## Introduction

These email sequences complement the basic templates and provide advanced follow-up strategies for different scenarios. Use these when standard templates aren't sufficient or when prospects need more sophisticated nurturing.

**Email Best Practices**:
- Subject lines should be compelling but not spammy
- Personalize with specific business details when possible
- Include clear, single call-to-action
- Mobile-optimize all content
- A/B test subject lines for better open rates

## Email Timing Guidelines

### Best Sending Times:
- **Tuesday-Thursday**: 10 AM - 2 PM (highest open rates)
- **Avoid Mondays**: People are catching up from weekend
- **Avoid Fridays**: People are focused on weekend plans
- **Avoid early morning/late evening**: Respect business hours

### Sequence Spacing:
- **WF-1**: Every 4-6 hours for first day, then daily
- **WF-2**: Every 24 hours
- **WF-4**: Every 2 hours during offer period
- **WF-6**: Monthly for first quarter, then quarterly

### A/B Testing Opportunities:
- Subject line urgency vs. benefit-focused
- Short vs. long email content
- Personal vs. professional tone
- Single vs. multiple call-to-action

---

**Remember**: Email is a supporting channel to phone calls, not a replacement. Use these sequences to increase phone contact success rates and maintain prospect engagement between calls.
